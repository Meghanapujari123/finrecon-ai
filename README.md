# 🚀 FinRecon AI

### AI Finance Controller for Reconciliation, Exception Investigation & Financial Intelligence

FinRecon AI is an AI-powered Finance Controller designed to automate financial reconciliation, identify transaction exceptions, investigate discrepancies using an LLM-powered agent, and keep humans in control of final financial decisions.

The system combines **deterministic financial reconciliation** with **AI-assisted investigation**, ensuring that financial numbers are computed from the underlying data rather than generated by an LLM.

# 💡 Problem Statement

Modern businesses process thousands of financial transactions across payment gateways, banks, invoices, and accounting ledgers.

Reconciling these records manually is:

- Time-consuming
- Error-prone
- Difficult to audit
- Difficult to scale
- Slow when investigating exceptions

Traditional reconciliation systems can identify that two records do not match, but they often do not explain:

> **Why did this mismatch happen?**

FinRecon AI addresses this gap by combining deterministic reconciliation with AI-powered investigation.
